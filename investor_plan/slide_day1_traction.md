# Day-1 Traction — A Funded Cash Engine, Not a Promise

> **We start with revenue.** A *firm* iDEX ESM/SIGINT production order funds the platform
> build-out from Day 1 — so equity does not fund every product line from zero.

## The order (firm, post-qualification)

- **Rs.105 Cr opening order book** — **Phase 1**: 5 systems × **Rs.21 Cr**, delivered over 12 months.
- Within a **firm Rs.210 Cr iDEX ESM MoQ** — **10 systems × Rs.21 Cr**, in **two 5-system phases**
  of **12 months each** (~24 months total). Phase 2 = a further Rs.105 Cr.
- **Development complete:** 4 iDEX milestones signed off; **first-of-production-model in build**.
- **Production-order revenue** — distinct from iDEX / TDF / Make-II grants, which the base case
  *excludes* and treats as separable upside (D-005).

## Where this sits on the commitment ladder

```
forecast → LOI → [ iDEX MoQ production order — post-qualification, first-article ] → multi-year series contract
                            ▲ we are here (4/4 dev milestones cleared)
```

Above an LOI or a forecast; the remaining step is series conversion, not contract existence.

## Why it matters — this is the flywheel's ignition

- The ESM line is **already part-developed and contracted**, so Day-1 cash funds AESA, counter-UAS,
  autonomous maritime, MALE ISR and Military AI **in parallel** — no re-equitising per product.
- It is the structural reason the equity rounds step **down** (Seed 450 → A 400 → B 250), not up.
- Paired with the Year-1 Military AI software line (93–94% gross margin), it turns Year-1 from a
  pure burn year into a revenue-and-gross-profit year.

## Single live risk (named, not hidden)

**Phase-1 → Phase-2 / series conversion *timing*.** A slip propagates to platform M1–M5 industrialisation
and to Series B sizing/pricing. Tracked against the funding flywheel (links to OQ-002 / Series B re-size,
OQ-003 / BG collateral).
