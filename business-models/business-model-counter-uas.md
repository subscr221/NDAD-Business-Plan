# Business Model — V3 Counter-UAS (Anti-Drone)

**Product code:** V3 · **Sheet:** `V3_CUAS` · **Domain:** Land / Air (RF + AI fusion) · **First MVP:** Year 2 · **Scale:** Year 3+  
**Parent plan:** Nitrodynamics Defense Platform · Sources: `03_strategy_and_products.md` §D; `V3_CUAS`; `Revenue` / `Backlog`

---

## 1. Overview & value proposition

Layered counter-drone systems combining radar, RF detection, EO/IR, AI fusion, and soft-kill (jamming) plus hard-kill effectors. Highest **unit-volume** product in the portfolio and the broadest Indian buyer base (services + paramilitary + critical infrastructure).

**Value proposition:** Integrator-grade C-UAS that reuses ESM RF/DSP and Military AI fusion — not a single-sensor jammer — so customers get a layered defense stack compatible with Nitrodynamics radar/EW/AI rather than a pure-play point solution.

---

## 2. Problem solved

- Proliferation of cheap drones against borders, airbases, ships, airports, and energy sites (Ukraine, Red Sea, 2019 Saudi infrastructure attacks as demand catalysts).
- Pure-play C-UAS vendors win on dedicated integration but lose when buyers need radar + EW + fusion in one award.
- Indian demand spans Army, IAF, Navy, paramilitary, and civil critical infrastructure — requiring three form factors (soldier, vehicle, fixed site).

## 5. Revenue model & pricing logic

- **Model:** Hardware system sales (kits / vehicles / fixed installations); optional V6 AI attach.
- **Recognition:** **33%** of opening backlog per year (slower burn than V1/V2) — `Backlog!D84`.
- **Pwin:** ~0.25 early → ~0.35 mid-horizon.
- **First recognized revenue:** Y3 (₹24 Cr); Y2 bookings begin (₹73 Cr) with zero Y2 recognition under 33% lag on empty prior backlog.

## 6. Cost structure & key drivers

| Driver | Detail |
| ------ | ------ |
| BoM | Sensors, RF jammer chains, EO/IR, compute, vehicle integration, fixed-site civils |
| Volume learning | CUAS-A/B hit BoM floor early (high unit counts) |
| Effector clearance | Soft-kill ships first; hard-kill munitions / WPC-DoT authorisations can lag (R-P-04) |
| Contract manufacturing | 30–40% of unit volume |
| AI attach COGS | Mostly in V6, not V3 BoM |

Y10 recognized BoM COGS ≈ ₹463 Cr on ₹1,037 Cr → **~55% GM (BoM only)** — consistent with BreakEven commentary that V3 is lowest-margin / highest-volume.

## 7. Go-to-market / channels

1. **MVP fielding Y2** using shared RF/DSP/AI; scale bookings Y2–Y3.
2. **Multi-channel India** — MoD services **and** non-MoD critical-infra (deliberate concentration hedge per R-S-01).
3. **Soft-kill-first** SKUs while hard-kill clearances proceed.
4. **Export** into the fastest-growing global defense sub-segment (25–30% CAGR).
5. **Bundle** with V1 radar cueing and V2 jammer heritage where tenders are integrated.
