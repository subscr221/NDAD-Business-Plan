## 1. Investment thesis

V2 is the **Day-1 cash engine** of the Nitrodynamics platform: a part-developed ESM/SIGINT line with a **firm ₹210 Cr iDEX production MoQ** (10 × ₹21 Cr, two 5-system phases). Y1 recognized revenue of **₹105 Cr** is 100% of consolidated workbook revenue and funds concurrent build-out of AESA, C-UAS, UAS, maritime, and AI. Capital into V2 is capital into *contracted production + RF/DSP maturity that every later vertical reuses* — not a speculative EW startup.

**Why now:** Indian EW remains import-heavy across Army (Samyukta/Himshakti), Navy (Sangraha/Varuna), and IAF pods; post-2022 EW demand is structurally re-rated; MoQ already converts policy into backlog.

## 3. Product & competitive position

**What is sold:** Four variants — naval ESM/ELINT (EW-A), aerial ESM pod (EW-B), tactical anti-drone jammer (EW-C), ground SIGINT station (EW-D). Y10 supporting units ~123. MoQ at concessional ₹21 Cr/system vs EW-A catalogue ₹30 Cr (~30% concession).

**Moat**

- Only line with **Day-1 contracted production backlog**.
- Full EW + SIGINT + tactical jammer family on one RF/DSP platform.
- Incumbency Pwin ~0.40–0.41 vs new-entrant lines ~0.25–0.35.
- Flywheel: every ESM shipment matures M1/M2 for V1 and V3.

Comparables: Saab, Elbit (integrated EW); broader specialist field for pods/jammers.

## 4. Business model snapshot

- **Mechanism:** Hardware programme sales; MoQ concessional, then catalogue.
- **Recognition:** 50% of opening backlog / year. Y1 opening backlog = ₹210 Cr → Y1 deliveries **₹105 Cr**.
- **Working capital:** Advances 15%; DSO 180 days; PBG 3% of backlog — material for this cash-engine line.
- **Detail:** Sibling business-model §§3–6.

## 9. Ask & returns framing

Investors buying into V2 exposure are buying **contracted Day-1 revenue + the RF platform flywheel**. Near-term return of capital is via MoQ delivery and follow-on EW bookings; strategic return is that V2 makes the rest of the portfolio fundable on ₹1,100 Cr equity.

**Gates:** Phase 1 delivery complete; Phase 2 on schedule; ≥1 follow-on award beyond MoQ by Y2–Y3; Pwin remains ~0.40; commercial ASP holds after concessional MoQ.
