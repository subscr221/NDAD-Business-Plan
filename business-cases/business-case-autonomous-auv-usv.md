## 1. Investment thesis

V5 completes the **sea domain** of the four-domain platform (Air / Land / Sea / Cyber) with software-defined maritime autonomy on **partner-built hulls** — asset-light versus a naval OEM. Product family: small mine-hunt/survey AUV, large ASW AUV, and mid-size USV (ISR / C-UAS host). Autonomy stack is shared with V6; sensors may pull M1/M2. Y10 recognized revenue **~₹549 Cr** at **~73% BoM GM** — strong hardware margins because software content is high and hull BoM is partner-optimised. Capital into V5 buys Navy MCM/ASW/harbour exposure and export optionality without yard capex.

**Why now:** Unmanned maritime is the “next wave” after aerial drones (USN Task Force 59, Ghost Shark, Magura precedents); Indian Navy needs AUVs for MCM/seabed warfare (Project 75 follow-on context; NSTL Vizag programmes).

## 3. Product & competitive position

**What is sold:** AUV-A (₹15 Cr Y1 ASP → ~₹12.5 Cr Y10), AUV-B large ASW (₹60 → ~₹51.2 Cr, from Y5), USV-A (₹45 → ~₹37.6 Cr). Y10 supporting units **~51**. Optional V3 C-UAS payload on USV-A; V6 autonomy/fusion standard.

**Moat vs peers**

| vs | Edge |
| -- | ---- |
| AUV specialists (Saab, Kongsberg, L3Harris, Anduril Dive-LD) | Trails pure maritime depth; wins on AI fusion, multi-domain sensors, C-UAS-host USV |
| Tier-1s | Faster unmanned cycles; lower overhead |
| Software-only autonomy startups | Ships vehicles + stack, not just licences |

Strategic role: **sea-domain completeness** for platform thesis and harbour-defense bundles (USV + V3 + V6).

## 6. Go-to-market & pipeline

1. Navy-first — MCM/seabed AUV-A entry; USV as C-UAS/ISR host; AUV-B as ASW flagship.
2. Wave-3 timing — ship from Y3 while V4 still in certification (concurrency thesis).
3. Bundle USV-A + V3 + V6 as harbour-defense package.
4. Export to allied navies seeking Task Force 59-like mass.
5. Dual shipyard partners by Y4 for hull competition and quality hedge.

## 8. Risks, mitigations & open diligence

| Risk | Residual | Mitigation |
| ---- | -------- | ---------- |
| Programme cost overrun (R-F-04) | Medium | 30% NRE contingency; partner hull competition |
| CM / shipyard quality (R-SC-04) | Low-Medium | Dual partners by Y4 |
| AI / autonomy safety (R-CY-03, R-R-01) | Medium | Human-on-the-loop for lethal ASW |
| Navy budget cyclicality | Medium | Multi-variant + export |

**Open diligence**

1. Named shipyard partners and hull cost vs plan.
2. Underwater autonomy performance inheritance from V6 (assumption: reuse works surface + subsurface).
3. AUV-B slip sensitivity — confirm AUV-A/USV cash bridge holds if ASW flagship delays.
4. Narrative Y5 ~₹180 Cr vs workbook Y5 recognized **₹100 Cr** — use workbook for diligence.

## 9. Ask & returns framing

Investors with V5 exposure buy **capital-efficient sea-domain optionality**: mid-teens category growth, high BoM GM, partner-hull asset light model, and strategic completeness for multi-domain awards.

**Gates:** First ship Y3; Navy reference by Y5; partner hull cost within contingency; V6 autonomy attach standard; export mid-horizon; 25% recognition rate remains valid (long maritime cycles).
