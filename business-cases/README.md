# Nitrodynamics — Product-Line Investor Business Cases

Decision-oriented investment memos for allocating capital into each of the six product verticals. These are **not** restatements of the business models; they answer *why fund this line, what returns/risks look like, and what gates unlock value*.

**Sibling docs:** [business-models/](../business-models/) (operating model detail) · Workbook `Defense_Platform_Business_Plan_Financial_V2.xlsx` · `investor_plan/`

**Currency:** INR Crore (₹ Cr). **Authoritative P&L revenue:** `Revenue` / `Backlog` deliveries — not V-sheet unit × ASP.

## How to use these cases

1. Read the **business model** for product packaging, variants, and operating KPIs.
2. Read the **business case** for capital-allocation, financial trajectory, ask framing, and diligence flags.
3. Prefer **workbook recognized revenue** over investor-narrative tables where they diverge (notably Y1 ₹105 vs ~₹53; V6 Y1 ship vs recognize; V1/V3/V4 Y10 narrative vs Backlog).
