## 1. Investment thesis

AESA is the natural second product off Nitrodynamics’ already-fielded ESM RF/DSP stack: three theatre variants (airborne X-band, ground S-band, naval X-band) share M1–M3 platform modules instead of three greenfield radar programmes. India is actively substituting imported mid-tier AESA with indigenous alternatives (Uttam as reference; Positive Indigenisation Lists), while tier-1 primes structurally under-serve programmes below ~USD 500M lifetime value. Capital into V1 buys a high-ASP, ~65% BoM-GM hardware franchise that reuses paid-for RF engineering and anchors multi-domain sensor bundles with V2/V6.

**Why now:** Import-replacement policy + fighter/drone/naval recapitalisation; ESM maturity compresses AESA time-to-field versus classic 5–7 year radar programmes.

## 3. Product & competitive position

**What is sold:** Qualified AESA systems (three variants) + typical V6 AI attach (edge / fusion). Catalogue ASP Y1 inputs ₹26 / ₹45 / ₹36 Cr; Y10 model ASPs ~₹21.8 / ₹37.6 / ₹30.1 Cr; ~95 supporting units/yr by Y10 (`V1_AESA`).

**Moat vs peers**

| Competitor class | Nitrodynamics edge |
| ---------------- | ------------------ |
| Tier-1 primes (NG, Raytheon, Leonardo, Saab, Thales) | Cycle time, cost-to-deliver, capital efficiency on mid-tier awards |
| RF specialists | Multi-domain integration + AI attach eligibility |
| Pure-plays | Can bid AESA inside bundled EW / C-UAS / AI awards |

Scorecard: Radio/AESA **4/4** (`MarketPositioning`). Reuse of ESM-proven M1/M2 is the structural differentiator.

## 4. Business model snapshot

- **Mechanism:** Fixed-price / programme hardware sales; bookings lead recognition ~12–18 months.
- **Recognition:** 50% of opening backlog / year (`Backlog!D82`).
- **Pricing:** Catalogue ASP with Wright’s-Law learning (0.92 / doubling, BoM floored at 70%).
- **Attach:** V6 Military AI on most awards; optional V2 ESM bundle.

### Capital / funding attribution

**No ring-fenced V1 equity line.** Platform NRE (~₹575 Cr cumulative M1–M5) and company equity (₹1,100 Cr Seed→B) are shared. V1 is primary consumer of **M1 RF** and **M2 DSP** (heavy Y1–Y3 platform spend: M1 ₹45+45+25 Cr; M2 ₹28+30+13 Cr). Series A (₹400 Cr, Y2) explicitly funds AESA prototype build; Series B (₹250 Cr, Y3) funds AESA production ramp. Anechoic/EMI facilities (capex wave) are shared qualification assets.

## 9. Ask & returns framing

Investors are not buying a standalone AESA OEM; they are buying **option value on a multi-band radar franchise** that amortises platform RF already paid by ESM, with Y10 recognized revenue ~₹865 Cr (17% mix), ~65% BoM GM, and ~₹1.9k Cr closing backlog.

**Gates for capital allocation into this line:** (1) M1/M2 productised; (2) first AESA contract signed (Series A gate); (3) first delivery Y2; (4) marquee win by Y5. Returns ride company IRR (~32% project) and platform reuse — V1 specifically is a mid-horizon cash contributor after V2, not the Day-1 engine.
