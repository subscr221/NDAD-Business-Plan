## 1. Investment thesis

Counter-UAS is the portfolio’s **volume and channel-diversity engine**: layered soft/hard-kill systems spanning soldier kits, vehicle mounts, and fixed sites, sold to MoD services *and* paramilitary / critical infrastructure. It reuses ESM RF/DSP and V6 fusion rather than competing as a jammer-only pure-play. At Y10, workbook recognized revenue reaches **~₹1,037 Cr (21% mix)** — second only to MALE — despite the **lowest hardware BoM GM (~55%)**. Capital into V3 buys high-growth category exposure (25–30% CAGR) plus a concentration hedge away from single-service MoD dependency.

**Why now:** Ukraine / Red Sea / infrastructure attacks re-rated C-UAS from optional to recapitalisation priority; India procures across Army, IAF, Navy, paramilitary, and civil infra simultaneously.

## 3. Product & competitive position

**What is sold:** CUAS-A dismounted (₹1.5 Cr Y1 ASP → ~₹1.25 Cr Y10), CUAS-B vehicle (₹14.25 → ~₹11.9 Cr), CUAS-C fixed site (₹65 → ~₹54.4 Cr). Y10 supporting units **~383** — portfolio volume leader. Architecture: sensor layer → AI fusion (M5/V6) → effector-agnostic engagement (soft-kill first).

**Moat vs peers**

| vs | Edge |
| -- | ---- |
| Pure-plays (DroneShield, Dedrone, Anduril Pulsar) | Trails pure integration score but wins on radar/EW/fusion bid eligibility |
| Tier-1s | Faster MVP; lower overhead on mid-tier awards |
| RF-only vendors | Full kill-chain + AI, not detection-only |

Strategic role: **volume + reference diversity** that EW/radar alone cannot provide.

## 4. Business model snapshot

### Capital attribution

Series A (₹400 Cr) funds **C-UAS production scale-up and MVP fielding** alongside ESM/AI. Series B funds concurrent multi-product ramp. V3 consumes M1/M2/M5; hard-kill clearances may lag — soft-kill SKUs are the cash bridge. Contract manufacturing (30–40% volume) keeps owned capex lighter than airframe lines. **Platform-shared costs** dominate; no V3-only equity tranche.

## 6. Go-to-market & pipeline

1. MVP fielding Y2 using shared RF/DSP/AI; scale bookings Y2–Y3.
2. Multi-channel India — MoD **and** non-MoD critical infra.
3. Soft-kill-first while WPC/DoT/hard-kill clearances proceed.
4. Export into fastest-growing global defense sub-segment.
5. Bundle with V1 radar cueing and V2 jammer heritage on integrated tenders.
