## Sheet: Strategy
|  | Strategic Narrative - New-Age Defense Prime |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  | Vision (10-yr) | Become the reference multi-domain defense technology firm of the new-age prime model - supplier of RF, autonomy and AI products across Air, Land, Sea and Cyber, anchored on a single, deeply reusable engineering platform and shipped on commercial-software cycle times. |
|  | Mission | Industrialise six pre-designed defense product lines on commercial-software cycle times. Convert owned IP and the iDEX-anchored ESM contract into a defensible new-age prime: lower NRE per product, faster qualification, compounding gross margin from Year 1. |
|  | New-age prime - operating model | Product-focused, software-defined, asset-light. Cross-functional product squads instead of program directorates. Short cycles (12–24 month MVP to field) replace multi-year legacy programs. Concurrent development across verticals while ESM (already partly developed at Day 1) is in production and sales - funding subsequent product lines from operating cash, not just equity. Capex deferred via partner anechoic / EMI / range facilities until volumes justify owned infra. |
|  | Operating thesis vs. legacy primes | Legacy primes carry the full NRE burden and the full bureaucracy overhead on every product, on 5–7 year cycles. This firm amortizes RF, DSP, SysEng, SW and AI investment across 6 product lines on 1–3 year cycles, with a productized ESM business funding adjacent products - driving lower NRE per product, faster qualification, and compounding gross margin from Year 1. |
|  | Core USP | Multi-domain autonomous + RF systems provider operating like a software company. One certifiable platform, six product lines fielded in 5 years, three domains. We out-cycle single-point vendors on cost-to-deliver and time-to-fielding, and out-scale narrow startups on capability breadth. |
|  | Strategic pillars | 1) Platform Engineering - five reusable tech modules (RF · DSP · SysEng · SW · AI).  2) Multi-Domain Coverage - Air (UAS, EW pods, AESA), Land (C-UAS, SIGINT, Mil AI), Sea (AUV, USV).  3) Concurrent Dev + Production - ESM cash funds adjacent product development.  4) IP + Sovereign Capability - cleared workforce + retained data rights + IP-led licensing.  5) Allied + FMS Export - DCS/FMS-eligible product variants from Day 1. |
|  | Capability waves (compressed) | Wave 1 (Y1-Y2): Deliver Rs.200 Cr iDEX ESM MoQ; ship V6 Military AI modules; V1 AESA + V3 C-UAS + V4 MALE prototype trials.  Wave 2 (Y3): V1 AESA first delivery + V3 C-UAS first orders + V4 MALE end-Y3 first delivery + V5 AUV first delivery.  Wave 3 (Y4-Y6): Production ramp across all six verticals + export/FMS variants.  Wave 4 (Y7+): Scale, replenishment cycles, allied bids. |
|  | Margin trajectory (model output) | Gross margin expands from ~25% (Y1, low BoM scale) toward 70–80% (Y10) as: (a) BoM learning, (b) platform reuse, (c) growing software/AI revenue mix. EBITDA crosses positive earlier than a legacy prime would achieve thanks to concurrent ESM revenue; FCF turns positive shortly after. |
|  | Funding strategy | Three stage-gated equity rounds totalling Rs.1,100 Cr (Seed 450, Series A 400, Series B 250) plus Rs.200 Cr venture/PSU debt and Rs.26.5 Cr iDEX/ADITI non-dilutive. BG cash margin 10% (existing facility) frees ~Rs.374 Cr of deployable cash vs legacy 100% margin assumption. No reliance on grant capital for survival - all milestones cash-self-sustaining post-Series B. |
|  | Key risks & mitigants | Schedule slip on hardware qualification (mitigant: small cert team + partner-lab leverage + MVP-first releases). Export-control restrictions (mitigant: dual variant ITAR/EAR99 builds, DCS workflow). Talent (mitigant: cleared-engineer pipeline + remote-first software roles + university programs). Customer concentration (mitigant: 6-vertical diversification + allied FMS pipeline from Y2). |
